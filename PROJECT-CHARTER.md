# Teacher Collaboration Lab — Project Charter

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Initial project scope approved; pilot and infrastructure pending  
**Geographic scope:** Georgia  
**Primary community language:** Georgian

## Mission
Build an open professional learning community for teachers across Georgia to strengthen digital collaboration, organized professional communication, and practical educational technology skills.

## Audience and Membership
- Audience: school teachers across Georgia, regardless of subject or starting digital proficiency.
- Membership is open to applications, with review followed by an invitation to the Slack community.
- The initial pilot targets approximately 10–20 teachers; this is a planning target, not confirmed enrollment.

## Objectives
1. Improve practical digital communication and collaboration.
2. Encourage structured, topic-focused professional discussions.
3. Support peer learning and responsible sharing of teaching resources.
4. Develop reusable training modules for collaborative tools and AI.

## Initial Pilot
**Working title:** Slack for Teacher Collaboration  
**Duration:** Four weeks

Topics: Slack fundamentals; channels and threads; professional communication; resource sharing; group activities; participant feedback.

## Project Systems
| System | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| ChatGPT Project | Planning, research, instructional design, coordination |
| GitHub | Public methodology, templates, versioned educational documentation |
| Google Drive | Learning resources and private administrative materials |
| Slack | Community interaction, practical learning, discussion and feedback |

Connecting and verifying these systems are separate project tasks. No integration is assumed operational until tested.

## Admission Workflow
1. Teacher submits a short application form.
2. Administrator reviews the application.
3. Approved applicant receives an invitation.
4. Participant completes onboarding.

Application records, contact details, private messages, student information, and confidential school records must not be published in this repository.

## Community Principles
- Respectful and constructive communication.
- Accessible, teacher-centered learning.
- Topic-focused discussions and clear moderation.
- Privacy and responsible data handling.
- Continuous improvement based on participant feedback.

## Pilot Evaluation
Assess onboarding, participation, practical task completion, effective use of channels and threads, ability to find and share resources, and participant feedback. Agree measurable targets before recruitment.

## Boundaries
- The pilot does not require schools to replace Messenger.
- Public access to educational documentation does not imply public access to Slack or administrative records.
- Expansion beyond the pilot requires a separate decision.

## Roadmap
1. Set up project instructions and documentation.
2. Configure and verify GitHub, Google Drive and Slack.
3. Develop the four-week curriculum and community rules.
4. Prepare applications, onboarding and recruitment.
5. Run the pilot.
6. Evaluate and improve.
