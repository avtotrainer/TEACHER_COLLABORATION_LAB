# Teacher Collaboration Lab

**A professional digital collaboration and learning community for teachers across Georgia.**

Teacher Collaboration Lab is an education-focused initiative designed to help teachers communicate more effectively, organize professional discussions, share teaching resources, and develop practical digital collaboration skills.

The community's working language is **Georgian**. Technical project documentation is maintained in English where practical.

## Who is it for?

School teachers across Georgia, regardless of subject area or prior experience with collaborative tools.

The community is open to applications. Participants join after their application is reviewed and an invitation is issued.

## Our goals

- Improve structured, topic-based professional communication.
- Encourage peer learning and responsible resource sharing.
- Build confidence using modern collaboration platforms.
- Develop practical, reusable professional learning modules.
- Explore Google Workspace, AI and other tools in future modules.

## First pilot: Slack for Teacher Collaboration

The initial pilot is planned as a **four-week practical program** for approximately **10–20 teachers**.

| Week | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Slack basics, joining a workspace, channels and messages |
| 2 | Threads, professional discussions, search and notifications |
| 3 | Resource sharing and collaborative activities |
| 4 | Practice, feedback and evaluation |

**Status:** Slack workspace setup is in progress. Participant recruitment has not started.

The pilot is intended to demonstrate better organization of professional communication, **not** to require schools to replace Messenger.

## Participation

The planned joining process is:

1. Submit a short application form.
2. Wait for the application review.
3. Receive an invitation if approved.
4. Complete community onboarding.
5. Read and acknowledge the Community Guidelines.

**Applications are not open yet.** A registration link will be added once the process and privacy notice are ready.

## Community principles

We value respectful communication, practical learning, accessibility, constructive feedback and protection of personal information. Participant applications, private messages, student records and confidential school information must not be posted in this public repository.

Participant-facing conduct expectations are defined in the [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY-GUIDELINES.md). Internal moderation principles, severity levels, escalation and incident-handling workflow are defined in the [Moderation Policy](MODERATION-POLICY.md).
